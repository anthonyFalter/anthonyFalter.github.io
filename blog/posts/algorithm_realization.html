Algorithm Realization

August 6, 2026, 6:46 AM · Ross Elopre

These past few weeks, I have been doing a lot of research on Regression Analysis and Statistics since I keep forgetting some technical terms that are crucial for understanding the underlying principles in my side projects. Upon diving in deeper and getting stuck in a rabbit hole of researching, I came across A LOT of information regarding model evaluation, key metrics used for determining the relationship between variables, and several types of regression analysis.

My research led me to learn the deeper parts of Simple Linear Regression, Multiple Linear Regression, and Logistic Regression. I also learned about the importance of understanding the assumptions of regression analysis and how to check for them especially in the context of real-world applications. There was too much information to unpack in one week, so I only focused on the crucial aspects of each analysis, especially ANOVA, R-squared, Adjusted R-squared, and the F-statistic.
