How we fared against DICT's 11th Startup Competition

November 12, 2026, 2:48 AM · Ross Elopre



Recently, we decided to give DICT's 11th Startup Competition a try in order to experience what founders experience when they pitch their ideas. As our very first startup competition, we were nervous during the initial event and events that soon followed. But we were able to learn a lot and gain valuable experience from this competition.

One of our key achievements during this competition was getting a placement in the top 20 of regional lineups, which for us, was a SIGNIFICANT accomplishment as first timers in these kinds of events.



Coming up with an innovation was already hard for us, but succesfully defining your value proposition was an even bigger challenge that we experienced that led us to search for creative ways to deliver our solution to our target market.

During our ideation stages, we have proposed several ideas that target sectors like agriculture, education and legal-tech. The reason we didn't choose agriculture is that is's highly saturated and as a team of newbies, we had to think outside the box to come up with a unique value proposition. What we ultimately ended up with was a Legal-Tech centered Software as a Service (SaaS) solution that services both civilians and lawyers.



We researched the market first and the specific pain points that both customers experience, and that is where we identified the gaps in the time taken to address these issues for lawyers and lack of access to legal resources for civillians.
