The Power of Statistics?

July 29, 2026, 8:49 AM · Ross Elopre

Image source: certification completion, by Ross Elopre, Hosted at imgur.com

In my recent blog post (Check Here), I have mentioned that I was taking the Google Advanced Data Analytics Certification. Well, I took this certification to bridge my gap in the foundational concepts of statistics, probability and Exploratory Data Analysis.

While I may already have the foundational knowledge for programming since I came from web development background, the statistical concepts require an entirely different discipline and approach. This led me to pursue courses related to Data Science in order to dive deeper and expand my knowledge, as well as sail away from web development and focus on big data instead.



This led me to Google's "Power of Statistics" course, which is a course that dives into foundational statistical concepts, specifically hypothesis testing, and descriptive statistics. For me, this was just what I needed to strengthen my understanding of these concepts, and apply it to my workflows. I especially like the way how I was introduced to A/B Testing, how A/B Testing works, and how it can impact decision-making in businesses, and in software development as well! (Like how we can compare different versions of a software, decide which performs better, and proceed with the better-performing version).



Image source: concept usage in action, by Ross Elopre, Hosted at imgur.com

This course helped me rethink the way I approach data analysis, by providing a more structured approach to interpreting data, rather than relying on intuition and whatever happens to be available.

I really enjoyed the topics of z-tests and t-tests, z-scores, and p-values. Since these are topics that I have already covered in my senior high, but never applied in a practical context aside from academic exercises. While the course was mostly theoretical, the modules were still informative and engaging, especially the video lectures so boredom is something you really don't have to worry about while taking this course.



Moving forward, I am eager to apply the concepts I have learned in this course to my personal projects, workflows and to my future career. And if you crave statistics, I REALLY do suggest to take this course as not only will it enhance your analytical skills, but it will also provide you with a solid foundation for more advanced data science topics which will help you in your future endeavors. That's all and godspeed to you!
